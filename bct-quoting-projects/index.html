Why this matters

Estimating is where bids are won and margin is protected. Quote too high and BCT loses the job; quote too low and a won job quietly loses money. Estimating error is the single biggest cause of margin erosion in construction — one bad estimate can wipe out the profit from several good jobs.

This service removes that risk two ways: a custom system that itemizes every material, rental, and labor line for BCT's concrete work, and hands-on quoting so bids go out fast, accurate, and defensible — without BCT carrying the cost of a full-time estimator (which runs $65K–$120K/year).
