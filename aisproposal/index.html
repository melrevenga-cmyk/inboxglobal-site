90-Day Engagement

A 90-day GTM engagement built for AIS's PE pipeline.

LinkedIn outreach, content, and outbound email — timed around AIS's June 22 Chicago trip, with a target of 2–4 qualified PE conversations over 12 weeks.

Prepared for
Jeff Mandell · AIS — Acquisition Integration Specialists · Florida
Build → Chicago → Review
01

Scope of work

A · LinkedIn

Presence, outreach, and content

  • Update the AIS company page — positioning, banner, services language
  • Create services page concept and copy for acquisitionintegrationspecialists.com, delivered to the website team in time for the June 22 launch
  • Connection requests and direct outreach to prospect list
  • Content posting based on the PMI Readiness Checklist diagnostic
  • Engagement (connect, follow, like, comment) on prospect activity before any cold email goes out, so the AIS name is familiar by the time outreach arrives
B · Email outbound

Cold email campaigns

  • Prioritize ~50 PE firms from Jeff's 4,000-contact list, then enrich and verify emails for the priority cohort
  • Outbound sent from pre-warmed mailboxes — the Chicago timing doesn't allow for a standard 2–4 week warmup, so emails go out from accounts with established sender reputation
  • Chicago cohort first — firms with Chicago presence or activity, sequenced around the June 22 trip as the reason to meet. Launches June 10, 12 days ahead of the trip so prospects have time to reply and book meetings during the visit
  • Florida and signal-based cohorts follow — built around AIS's Florida base and on add-on activity signals, layered in late June through July to reach the full 50-firm target
  • Reply triage, meeting booking, and follow-up sequencing throughout
02

Timeline

June 1 — June 9

Setup & warming

List validation and enrichment. Email sequence drafted and tested. Company page updates live. Services page concept delivered to website team. LinkedIn warming begins by June 3 — connection requests and engagement on prospect activity.

June 10 — July 10

Outreach & Chicago

Chicago cohort launches June 10. Meetings book through the back half of June and during the Chicago trip (June 22 onward). Florida and signal-based cohorts roll out late June through July to fill the 50-firm target. LinkedIn content cadence active throughout. Reply triage and meeting confirmation handled in real time.

July 11 — Aug 31

Iterate & document

If signal is strong, additional cohorts run in July and August beyond the initial 50. Content cadence continues. The full system — list, sequences, content playbook — is documented for AIS to keep running. Day 90 review on whether the engagement continues into a fractional role.
