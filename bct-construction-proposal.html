The situation

BCT is established — the business isn't the problem. Years of completed projects and working relationships already do the heavy lifting. The constraint is purely visibility: when a GC, developer, or procurement team goes looking for BCT online, there's almost nothing to find.

That absence costs bids. More owners now run a quick search before shortlisting, and a contractor with no website, no company page, and no profile on the platforms where projects get sourced reads as smaller or less active than BCT actually is.

The fix is focused, not flashy. This isn't a brand overhaul. It's the minimum credible footprint required to be found, vetted, and trusted: a real domain, a tight four-page site that shows the work, a professional LinkedIn presence, and a foothold on the bidding platforms that matter.

Everything here is designed so that within a few weeks, BCT can hand a prospective client a link instead of an explanation.

01

The work packages

Four deliverables, sequenced so each one builds on the last. Pricing is handled separately so we can walk through scope first and adjust before any numbers are attached.

Foundation

Domain selection & purchase

Securing the right web address is the foundation everything else sits on — the website, the email, and the LinkedIn link all point back to it. I'll shortlist strong, professional options, walk you through the trade-offs, and register the one you choose.

  • Curated shortlist of available domains
  • Recommendation tied to how BCT brands
  • Registration and ownership in your name
  • Matching professional email address ready to set up

You keep full ownership of the domain from day one — it's registered to BCT, not to me.

Storefront

Four-page website with project gallery

A clean, fast, mobile-ready site that does one job: make BCT look as established online as it is in the field. Four pages keep it focused and easy to maintain, with the project gallery doing most of the selling.

  • Home — who BCT is, at a glance
  • Services — the work BCT takes on
  • Project gallery — images & video you provide
  • Contact — clear way to start a conversation
  • Mobile and desktop layouts
  • Built to add pages as BCT grows

You supply the project photos and videos; I handle layout, sequencing, and making the gallery present the work at its best.

Credibility

LinkedIn company presence

For B2B construction work, LinkedIn is where decision-makers verify that a company is real and active. A complete, well-presented page turns a quick background check into a point in BCT's favor.

  • Company page setup, fully completed
  • Logo and branded banner
  • Services section written for the audience
  • Consistent with the website's look and message

Set up so it's ready to grow — you or your team can post updates and project wins over time with no extra build work.

Pipeline

Bidding platforms — trial, selection & monitoring

This is where the digital presence starts paying for itself. I'll get BCT onto the construction-bidding platforms where private-sector projects are posted — the sites that list general contractors, subcontractors, and project requirements categorized by specialty and scope — and trial the ones worth trialing so we can see which actually surface work BCT can win.

  • Profiles set up to match BCT's specialties
  • Filtering for civil & build work BCT does
  • Focus on private-sector, non-union projects
  • Scoped to a 30-mile radius of Chicago / Naperville
  • Trial period to test real lead quality
  • Monitoring & a recommendation on what to keep

BCT's wheelhouse drives the setup: storm drainage, street layout, excavation, concrete, carpentry/framing, and drywall — applied to residential buildings, professional buildings, and clinics. We steer toward private work and away from public, unionized projects to keep the pipeline clean and worth your time.
